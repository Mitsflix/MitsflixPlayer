# Mitsflix Player — Mitsflix-only updates

OwnTV's updates are listed in CHANGELOG.md. Updates made only to Mitsflix Player use a fourth
number on top of the OwnTV version they're built on (v5.2.1.1, v5.2.1.2, …) and are listed here.
The bullets under each version are what the app shows as "What's new".

## v5.2.2
- **Picture-in-picture:** keep watching in a small window when you leave the app
- **Voice search** on the Search screen
- **Live TV:** the preview keeps playing while you browse categories, and the app can start on your last channel
- **Smoother** TV guide and Home scrolling, and tidier channel names
- **Category locks** with their own PIN, and a Settings lock per profile
- **Sports:** scores, calendar, league tables, fixtures and team pages, with 54 more leagues
- Plus everything from earlier Mitsflix updates

## v5.2.1.4
- **Phones & tablets: tap a movie** to see its details — artwork, plot, cast — with Play, Favourite, Trailer, Download and more
- **Phones & tablets: tap the screen while watching** to show or hide the controls (seek, next, subtitles, audio)
- **Double-tap the left or right side** to skip back or forward in movies and episodes

## v5.2.1.3
- **Categories now stay on screen** beside the channel list in Live TV, Movies and Series
- Prefer them tucked away? Settings › Layout › Live TV layout › Stage, and Movies & Series layout › Cinematic

## v5.2.1.2
- **Phones & tablets:** tap a playlist to Edit, Re-sync or Delete it, and tap any settings row to see its options
- **Phones & tablets:** Movies and Series start straight with the posters, no empty space at the top
- **Phones & tablets:** the app stays in landscape, and the Settings page scrolls to its last row
- **Now Trending** on Home is built straight away with movie info from TMDB

## v5.2.1.1
- **New Mitsflix look: black and gold** — warm black backgrounds and the Mitsflix gold accent throughout
- **Mitsflix logo on the setup screens**
- The accent colour "Mitsflix Gold" is the default; the other colours are still in Settings › Appearance
- **TV guide added automatically** when you add a playlist — no more "Sync guide?" question
- **More on Home:** Recent channels is now shown, and movie info and Now Trending come straight from TMDB
- **Cloudflare DNS on by default** for more reliable connections (change it in Settings › DNS)
- Small wording fixes
